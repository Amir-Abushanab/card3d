Scenes and AR

environment lights the card with an HDRI (one of the bundled ids, or a .hdr URL) and floor stands it on a reflective floor with a contact shadow. Both load on first use, from a chunk next to the bundle. ar adds a View in AR button where the device has an AR viewer (iPhone, iPad; Android with ar-src, a hosted .glb); the exporter loads only when it is tapped.
