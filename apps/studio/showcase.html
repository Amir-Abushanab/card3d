card3d
Cards Source Open the studio

3D metal cards, designed in your browser. Metals, foils, effects and shaders, live.

One canvas

Seven card slots recycle along an endless strip, one card per studio preset. Every card is live 3D: metal, foil, effects and shader layers.

Drift, drag, fling

Drag or scroll sideways to scrub the strip, let go to fling it. Click a card to bring it to the centre and turn it over.

Seeded order

Add ?seed=42 for a reproducible shuffle, ?card=gold-shine (any preset id) to pin one design to the centre, or ?lighting=warm to pick the light.

Light it

Six lighting presets (studio, dramatic, warm, cool, dark and light) retune the softboxes, the key light and the backdrop. The studio saves the choice with each design and exports it.

Your mark, your type

Upload an SVG or PNG logo to replace the corner mark (or place it elsewhere on the face), and set the name in one of six open-licence fonts that ship with the engine.

Make your own

Open the studio to design a card from scratch, then export an image, a turntable video or GIF, a glTF model or code.
